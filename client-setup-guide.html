Before You Start

1
Phase 1

Resend Email Service Setup

Resend handles all email sent from your Audela website — form confirmations, contact notifications, and demo request confirmations. It's a modern email API with 3,000 free emails/month.

Step-by-Step Setup

1

Create Your Resend Account

Go to resend.com and click "Sign Up". Use your company email. Verify your email address via the confirmation link.

2

Verify Your Domain (Optional but Recommended)

In Resend dashboard, go to Domains → Add Domain. Enter your domain (e.g., audela.me). Resend will give you 3 DNS records to add to your domain registrar. Add these records in your domain provider (GoDaddy, Namecheap, CloudFlare, etc.). Wait 5–10 minutes for DNS to propagate.

About Domain Verification

Without verification: Emails sent from onboarding@resend.dev (test address). Good for testing. With verification: Emails sent from your domain (e.g., hello@audela.me). Required for production.

3

Generate Your API Key

In Resend dashboard, go to API Keys → Create Key. Name it Audela Website. Copy the key — you'll need this next.

4

Add API Key to Vercel (Production)

Go to your Vercel project → Settings → Environment Variables. Add a new variable: RESEND_API_KEY with your API key as the value. Save.

Sender Address (Test)
onboarding@resend.dev
Sender Address (Production)
hello@audela.me (after domain verification)
Recipient Email
Your company email (e.g., contact@audela.me)

Resend Setup Complete

You can now send transactional emails from your website forms. Move to Phase 2: Supabase.

2
Phase 2

Supabase Database Setup

Supabase is your database. It stores all contact form submissions, demo requests, and visitor information. You'll access this via an admin dashboard to see who contacted you.

Step-by-Step Setup

1

Create Your Supabase Account

Go to supabase.com and click "Start your project". Sign up with your company email. Verify your email.

2

Create a New Project

Click New Project. Name it audela-contacts. Choose a region closest to you (e.g., us-east-1). Set a strong database password (save this somewhere secure). Click Create new project.

Supabase will take 1–2 minutes to set up. Wait for the project to finish initializing.

3

Create the "contacts" Table

In Supabase, go to SQL Editor (left sidebar) → New Query. Paste this SQL code:

CREATE TABLE contacts (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT uuid_generate_v4(),
  name TEXT NOT NULL,
  company TEXT,
  email TEXT NOT NULL,
  product TEXT,
  timing TEXT,
  message TEXT,
  contact_type TEXT,
  created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT NOW()
);

Click Run. The table is now created.

4

Get Your API Keys

Go to Settings (bottom left) → API. Copy your Project URL and anon key. You'll need these for the website.

5

Add Keys to Vercel

In Vercel project settings → Environment Variables, add:

  • SUPABASE_URL = your Project URL
  • SUPABASE_ANON_KEY = your anon key
✓ Project Name
audela-contacts
✓ Table Name
contacts
✓ Auto-Generated Fields
id (UUID), created_at (timestamp)

Supabase Ready

Your database is now live. Forms will save submissions automatically. Move to Phase 3 to access your admin dashboard.

3
Phase 3

Admin CRM Dashboard

Your admin dashboard lets you see all contact submissions, filter by type (demo requests vs. inquiries), export data, and manage responses. It's built into Supabase and accessible via the web.

Accessing Your Admin Dashboard

1

Log Into Supabase

Go to supabase.com and click Sign In. Use your account email and password.

2

Open Your Project

Click your audela-contacts project from the dashboard.

3

View the Contacts Table

In the left sidebar, click Table Editor → contacts. All form submissions appear here as rows.

4

Filter & Search

Use the filter icon to show only:

  • Demo requests: contact_type = 'demo'
  • General inquiries: contact_type = 'contact'
  • Last 7 days: created_at > now - interval '7 days'
5

Export Data

Right-click on any row → Export → CSV to download submissions for reports or follow-up.

What You'll See in Each Submission

Field Description name Full name of the person who submitted the form company Their organization or company name email Email address for follow-up product Which Audela solution they're interested in (Clara, Reven, etc.) timing When they're ready to engage (ASAP, this week, flexible, etc.) message Their full inquiry or message contact_type demo = demo request | contact = general inquiry created_at Date and time the form was submitted
Pro Tip

Set up email alerts in Supabase: Go to Settings → Notifications to receive an email every time someone submits a form.

Admin Access Live

You can now monitor all contact submissions in real time. Move to Phase 4 to set up your LinkedIn company page.

4
Phase 4

LinkedIn Company Page Setup

Your LinkedIn company page is the central hub for B2B visibility. It builds credibility, allows followers to stay updated, and can be connected to your Audela website for social sharing.

Step-by-Step Setup

1

Go to LinkedIn Company Pages

Go to linkedin.com → Log in with your work account. Click Work (top left) → Create a Company Page.

2

Fill Company Information

Enter your company details:

  • Company name: Audela
  • Website: audela.me
  • Company size: 6–50 employees (or your actual size)
  • Industry: Software Development / Artificial Intelligence
  • Company type: For-profit
3

Upload Company Logo & Banner

Use your Audela logo (PNG or JPG). Banner should be 1200×627px. Keep branding consistent with your website.

Logo Requirements

Minimum 200×200px, preferably 400×400px. PNG with transparent background works best.

4

Write Compelling "About" Section

Example: "Audela builds specialized AI solutions for industries where decisions have consequences. We serve healthcare, finance, logistics, and more with purpose-built, not adapted, AI."

Keep it concise (max 2–3 sentences). Include your mission statement.

5

Add Specialties

List your core competencies (e.g., "AI Solutions", "Finance Automation", "Healthcare Technology", "Workforce Intelligence").

6

Verify Your Company

LinkedIn will send a verification link to your company email. Click it to verify ownership. This unlocks more features like LinkedIn ads and analytics.

Best Practices for LinkedIn Company Page

  • Post regularly: 1–2 posts per week keep your page active and visible in the feed
  • Share industry news: Post about AI trends, industry updates, and company milestones
  • Engage with followers: Respond to comments and messages promptly
  • Add team members: Encourage employees to add Audela as their current company on their profiles
  • Use LinkedIn articles: Write longer-form content on LinkedIn to establish thought leadership
  • Monitor analytics: Check your Page Analytics to see what content resonates

LinkedIn Ready

Your company page is now live and can be shared from your Audela website. Move to Phase 5 to set up your Twitter/X business account.

5
Phase 5

Twitter/X Business Account Setup

Twitter/X is where industry leaders and tech professionals gather. A business account for Audela amplifies your thought leadership, announces product updates, and engages with potential customers.

Step-by-Step Setup

1

Create a Twitter/X Account

Go to twitter.com and click Create account. Use a professional email (e.g., hello@audela.me).

2

Choose Your Username

Recommended: @AudelaAI or @BuildAudela. Keep it short, brandable, and professional.

3

Complete Your Profile

  • Profile picture: Your Audela logo
  • Header image: 1500×500px branded hero image
  • Bio: "Specialized AI for industries where decisions matter. Building purpose-built AI solutions."
  • Website: https://audela.me
4

Switch to Business Account

Go to Settings & Privacy → Your Account → Account Type → Switch to Business Account. This unlocks analytics, verified badge eligibility, and business features.

5

Apply for Twitter Verification (Optional)

Go to Settings → Verification & Badges → Get Verified. Provide documentation of your company (business registration, website proof). Verification can take 1–4 weeks.

6

Set Up Twitter API Access (Advanced)

If you want to auto-post or track mentions: Go to developer.twitter.com → Apps → Create App. Name it "Audela Website". Get your API keys from the Keys & Tokens section.
